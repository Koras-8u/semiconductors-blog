Electronic Devices

PN Junction

Thermal Equilibrium: Implies that no external forces such as voltages, electric fields, magnetic fields, or temperature gradient are acting on the semiconductor.

Thermal-equilibrium electron concentration

In conduction band

\begin{equation} n_0 = N_c e^\left( \frac{ -(E_c - E_F) }{ k_BT } \right) \end{equation}

In valence band

\begin{equation} p_0 = N_v e^\left( \frac{ -(E_F - E_v) }{ k_BT } \right) \end{equation}

Effective density of states

In conduction band

$$ N_c = 2 \left( \frac{ 2\pi m^*_nk_BT }{h^2} \right)^{3/2} $$

In valence band

$$ N_v = 2 \left( \frac{ 2\pi m^*_pk_BT }{h^2} \right)^{3/2}$$

Built-in Potential Barrier

\begin{equation} V_{bi} = V_tln \left( \frac{N_aN_d}{n^2_i} \right) \end{equation} where \( V_t = \frac{ k_BT }{ q } = 0.026V \) is defined as the thermal voltage.

Electric Field in the depletion region

In the P region

\begin{equation} E(x) = \frac{ -qN_a }{ \epsilon_s }( x + x_p ), \quad -x_p \leq x \leq 0 \end{equation}

In the N region

\begin{equation} E(x) = \frac{ -qN_d }{ \epsilon_s }( x_n - x ), \quad 0 \leq x \leq x_n \end{equation}

In the metallurgical junction

\begin{equation} E(x=0) = \frac{ -qN_d }{ \epsilon_s }x_n = \frac{ -qN_a }{ \epsilon_s }x_p \end{equation} \begin{equation} \implies N_dx_p = N_dx_n \end{equation}

Potential in the depletion region

In the P region

\begin{equation} \phi_p(x) = \frac{qN_a}{2\epsilon_s}(x + x_p)^2 \end{equation}

In the N region

\begin{equation} \phi_n(x) = \frac{qN_d}{\epsilon_s}\left(x_nx - \frac{x}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{qN_a}{2\epsilon_s}x_p^2 \end{equation}

In the metallurgical junction

\begin{equation} \phi(x=0) = \frac{qN_a}{2\epsilon_s}x_p^2 \end{equation}

The built-in potencial

\begin{equation} \phi(x=x_n) = \frac{qN_a}{2\epsilon_s}(N_ax_p^2 + N_dx_n^2) \end{equation}
