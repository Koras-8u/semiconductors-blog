PN Junction

Thermal Equilibrium: Implies that no external forces such as voltages, electric fields, magnetic fields, or temperature gradient are acting on the semiconductor.

Thermal-equilibrium electron concentration

In conduction band

\begin{align} n_0 & = \int g_c(E) f_F(E) dE \\ & = N_c e^\left( \frac{ -(E_c - E_F) }{ k_BT } \right) \end{align}

In valence band

\begin{align} p_0 & = \int g_v(E) \left[ 1 - f_F(E) \right] dE \\ & = N_v e^\left( \frac{ -(E_F - E_v) }{ k_BT } \right) \end{align}

Effective density of states

In conduction band

$$ N_c = 2 \left( \frac{ 2\pi m^*_nk_BT }{h^2} \right)^{3/2} $$

In valence band

$$ N_v = 2 \left( \frac{ 2\pi m^*_pk_BT }{h^2} \right)^{3/2}$$

Built-in Potential Barrier

\begin{align} V_{bi} & = \frac{ k_BT }{ e }ln \left( \frac{N_aN_d}{n^2_i} \right) \\ & = V_tln \left( \frac{N_aN_d}{n^2_i} \right) \end{align} where \( V_t = \frac{ k_BT }{ e } \) is defined as the thermal voltage.

Electric Field in the depletion region

In the P region

$$ E = \frac{ -eN_a }{ \epsilon_s }( x + x_p ) $$ for \( -x_p \leq x \leq 0 \)

In the N region

$$ E = \frac{ -eN_d }{ \epsilon_s }( x_n - x ) $$ for \( 0 \leq x \leq x_n \)

In the metallurgical junction

$$ N_dx_p = N_dx_n $$
