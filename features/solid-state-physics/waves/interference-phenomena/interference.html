Interference conditions

The condition for intensity maximum is \begin{equation} n\lambda = a\sin \theta \quad (n = 0, 1, 2, 3,...) \end{equation} while for a minimum, \begin{equation} \left( n + \frac{1}{2} \right) \lambda = a\sin \theta \quad (n = 0, 1, 2, 3,...) \end{equation} where \( n \) is the order of interference, \( \lambda \) the wavelength, \( a \) the distance between the slits and \( a\sin\theta \) is the path difference between rays comming from the adjacent slits.

Distance between fringes

The distance \( z \) between the interference maxima and minima and the central point of the screen is \begin{equation} z = L\tan\theta \end{equation} where \( L \) is the normal distance from the slits to the screen.
