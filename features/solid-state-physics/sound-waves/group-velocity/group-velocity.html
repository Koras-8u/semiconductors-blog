Group velocity

The group velocity is the phase velocity of a long-wavelegth envelope, which is also a wave, formed by two (or even more) sinusoidal waves of slightly different frequency and wavelength. \begin{equation} v_g = \frac{d\omega}{dk} \end{equation}

  • The energy of the wave is propagated with the group velocity
  • For elastic waves in a homogeneous, isotropic medium, the phase and group velocity are identical.
To demonstrate such a fact, let there be two sinusoidal wave trains having the same amplitude, but sligthly different frequencies and wavelengths. \begin{align} u_1 & = A\cos(kx - \omega t) \\ u_2 & = A\cos[(k + dk)x + (\omega + d\omega)t] \end{align} Superposing these wave, we obtain \begin{align} u_1 + u_2 & = A\cos(kx - \omega t) + A\cos[(k + dk)x + (\omega + d\omega)t] \\ & = A\{\cos(kx - \omega t) + \cos[(k + dk)x + (\omega + d\omega)t]\} \\ & = A(\cos \alpha + \cos \beta) \end{align} From elementary trigonometry \begin{equation} \cos \alpha + \cos \beta = 2\cos\left[\frac{1}{2}(\alpha + \beta)\right]\cos\left[\frac{1}{2}(\alpha - \beta)\right] \\ \end{equation} while from the superposition, we have \begin{align} \alpha + \beta & = kx - \omega t + [(k + dk)x - (\omega + d\omega)t] \\ & = 2kx - 2\omega t + xdk - td\omega \\ & \cong 2(kx - \omega t) \end{align} and \begin{align} \alpha - \beta & = kx - \omega t - [(k + dk)x - (\omega + d\omega)t] \\ & = td\omega - xdk\\ \end{align} In the sum of angles, the terms containing differential can be neglected in comparison with the others, though in the substraction the differential quantitites are significant since there are no others terms. So, finally we have \begin{align} u_1 + u_2 & = 2A\cos(kx - \omega t)\cos\left(\frac{1}{2}td\omega - \frac{1}{2}xdk\right) \\ & = 2\cos\left(\frac{1}{2}td\omega - \frac{1}{2}xdk\right)u_1 \end{align} The equation of motion of the point zero phase of the envelope wave is \begin{equation} td\omega - xdk = 0 \end{equation} \begin{equation} \Rightarrow x = \frac{d\omega}{dk}t = v_gt \end{equation} from which we define the group velocity.
