Sound waves and lattice vibrations

Cohesive energy in ionic crystal

Ionic substances are bound together by electrostatic forces between the cations and anions formed by the transfer of valence electrons. But it is also true that there is a repulsive force which begins to act between neighboring atoms when their electron clouds start to overlap, and increases very rapidly until it balances any attractive forces that are present.

The total energy of interaction for a crystal containing 2N ions is \begin{equation} U_0 = - \frac{N \alpha q^2}{4 \pi \epsilon _0 a} \left(1 - \frac{1}{b} \right) \end{equation} which certainly represents the equilibrium configuration of a ionic lattice.

Forces on vibrating atoms

For a one-dimensional dynamical model of a crystal, viewed as a line of equal masses coupled together with ideal springs, the Hooke's-law force constant in the n-th atom due to its first-neighbors is \begin{equation} \beta _1 = \frac{(4.93)q^2}{4\pi \epsilon _0 a^3} \end{equation}
