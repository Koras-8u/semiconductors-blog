X-ray scattering from two lattice points

O and P are separated by the lattice vector RL. Both scatter the same incident wave along nf. Adjust the geometry and watch the scope on the right: when the two scattered waves line up crest-to-crest you get a diffraction peak; when they land crest-to-trough they cancel.
