Problem 3

From the data in the above table, calculate the interatomic spacing (distance AB in Figure 1.7) in crystalline silicon. How many atoms are there per unit volume \( [cm^{-3}] \)?

From the table, we have \( a = 5.42 \AA \). It must be known that the bond length AB in the diamond structure is equal to one-quarter of the cubic cell diagonal, i.e., \( AB = \frac{1}{4}d \), where \( d = |\vec{d}| \), and \( \vec{d} = a(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) \) as the cell diagonal.

Then, we can calculate \begin{align} AB & = \frac{1}{4}\sqrt{a^2 + a^2 + a^2} \\ & = \frac{1}{4}\sqrt{3a^2} \\ & = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a \end{align} By substituting the value for \( a \), we have \begin{align} AB & = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}(5.42 \AA) \\ & = 2.34 \AA \end{align} Now, let's calculate the atoms per unit volume with the help of \( n = \frac{N_{uc}}{V_{uc}} \) as follows: \begin{align} V_{uc} & = a^3 \\ & = (5.42 \AA)^3 \\ & = 159.22 \AA^3 \left( \frac{10^{-30}m^3}{\AA^3} \right) \left( \frac{10^6cm^3}{m^3} \right) \\ & = 159.22 \times 10^{-24}cm^3 \end{align} There are eight atoms in the unit cell, \( n_{uc} = 8 \), therefore \begin{align} n & = \frac{8}{159.22 \times 10^{-24}cm^3} \\ & = 5.024 \times 10^{22}cm^{-3} \end{align} Finally we say that the concentration of atoms in crystalline silicon is \( 5.024 \times 10^{22}cm^{-3} \).
