Atom concentration

The atom concentration \( n \) is the number of atoms per unit volume (most of the time in \( [cm^{-3}] \)): \begin{equation} n = \frac{N_{uc}}{V_{uc}} \end{equation} where \( N_{uc} \) is the number of atoms within the unit cell and \( V_{uc} \) the volume of the unit cell, related to the cell edges.
