Electromagnetism

Coulomb's Law

$$ \vec{ F }_{12} = k_e \frac{ q_1q_2 }{ r^2 } \hat{r} $$

Electric Field

$$ \vec{ E } = \frac{ \vec{ F}_e }{ q_0 } $$

Electronic Devices

Thermal-equilibrium electron concentration in the conduction band

\begin{align} n_0 & = \int g_c(E) f_F(E) dE \\ & = N_c e^\left( \frac{ -(E_c - E_F) }{ k_BT } \right) \end{align}

Thermal-equilibrium hole concentration in the valence band

\begin{align} p_0 & = \int g_v(E) \left[ 1 - f_F(E) \right] dE \\ & = N_v e^\left( \frac{ -(E_F - E_v) }{ k_BT } \right) \end{align}

Effective density of states in the conduction band

$$ N_c = 2 \left( \frac{ 2\pi m^*_nk_BT }{h^2} \right)^{3/2} $$

Effective density of states in the valence band

$$ N_v = 2 \left( \frac{ 2\pi m^*_pk_BT }{h^2} \right)^{3/2}$$
